# Amazing Soft

Amazing Soft is a software company in Bucharest, Romania, founded in January 2016. We build AI automation, mobile apps, brand identities, websites and custom software for companies that need things to work for years, not just at launch.

We have clients in Romania and elsewhere in Europe, in Canada, the United States and the United Arab Emirates. We work remotely, in English and Romanian: a nearshore team for companies in the UK and the EU, and a remote partner for companies in North America and the Gulf.

## What we build

- **AI automation:** AI agents for customers, document and operations workflows (invoices, orders, contracts), and AI added to the systems a company already runs, with a person validating where it matters.
- **Mobile apps:** iOS and Android in Flutter, Swift and Kotlin, including Apple Watch and Wear OS. We have published on the App Store and Google Play since 2016.
- **Branding:** identity and logo, branding for digital products, campaign assets, photo and video.
- **Websites and custom software:** WordPress, WooCommerce and Next.js websites, custom web and desktop software, Google Ads and SEO, and monthly maintenance and support.

## How we work

- You work directly with the people who decide and build, and after launch we stay with you.
- We build in versions you see and test along the way, not only at the end.
- We hand over the domain, the hosting, the accounts, the files and the documentation.
